and_Lubrifont is Studio ANDhitektura's edition of WD-XL Lubrifont, a geometric display font with rounded-square forms and a full Chinese and Japanese character set. This edition extends it for technical and architectural documents in Macedonian and other European languages: Cyrillic and Central European letters, a dotted zero and diagonal seven for legibility, tabular figures, fractions, super- and subscripts, technical symbols, kerning, and a Latin / Cyrillic / Greek family in Light, Regular and Bold.

Based on WD-XL Lubrifont by NightFurySL2001 and ZCOOL QingKe HuangYou. To contribute or raise an issue, please visit the GitHub repository.
